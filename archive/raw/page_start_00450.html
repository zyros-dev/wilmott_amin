when we consider ode we always should state the area where solutions should be defined. The standard sufficient conditions for existence of the solution are continuous in t on [ 0 , T ] and boundness of the continuous first order derivative  in y on [$] [ \,0 \,, T\,] \times ( - \infty , + \infty ) [$]. These conditions are classical and they are sufficient for the convergence of the Picard iterations too. Of course iterations and solution of the odes can be constructed under more general conditions too.
Maybe it's a language thing, or maybe you've never had any mathematical training. I suspect mostly the latter. Let's suppose that there are levels to this problem, like levels in a computer game. You haven't got through Level 1 yet. And this particular game is about the easiest there is.
