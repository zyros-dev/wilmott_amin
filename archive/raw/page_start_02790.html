Friends, here is my strategy to find the Coefficients of a multi-dimensional Hermite-Series.

Estimation procedure for Multi Dimensional Joint or Conditional Hermite-Series/Z-Series From Joint Observation Data of Multiple Random Variables.

Suppose, we have a random variable Y that depends upon random variables U, V, W and X and we want to capture all this dependence structure mathematically using multi-dimensional Hermite-Series/ZSeries. We would have to find \( \left[  Y\,  \,|X,W,V,U \right](Z_y \, , \, Z_x,\,Z_w\,,Z_v,\, Z_u))\, \,\) as pointed in previous post.

Suppose we start our calculations with first estimating the effect of X on Y. We would have to find the effect of X on other variables. For this, we use our 2D calibration procedures to find the value of Y, W, V and U conditional on X. This will be in the following form for Y (The case of W, V and U would be exactly similar).

\begin{align}
\left[  Y\,  \,|X \right]&(Z_y \, , \, Z_x))\, \,\\
&=\, \Big[\, cH_{0,0} \, + \, cH_{0,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{0,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \,\, + \, cH_{0,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\,\Big] \\
&+\, \Big[ \, cH_{1,0} \, + \, cH_{1,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{1,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \,\, + \, cH_{1,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_1(Z_y) \, \\
&+\, \Big[ \, cH_{2,0} \, + \, cH_{2,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{2,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \,\, + \, cH_{2,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_2(Z_y) \, \\
&+\, \Big[ \, cH_{3,0} \, + \, cH_{3,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{3,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \, \,+ \, cH_{3,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_3(Z_y) \, \\
&+\, \Big[ \, cH_{4,0} \, + \, cH_{4,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{4,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \,\, + \, cH_{4,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_4(Z_y) \, \\
&+\, \Big[ \, cH_{5,0} \, + \, cH_{5,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{5,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \, \,+ \, cH_{5,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_5(Z_y) \, \\
\end{align} 


After fitting the above conditional version of Y on X, We sort the data of Y and line it in an increasing order. This way, we can assign a  \(Z_y\)  to every data value. We remove the effect of Zx based hermite polynomials from Y|X by subtracting them from Y|X.  The value being subtracted is given below (we could do this since, we know \(Z_y\) and \(Z_x\) associated with each data entry after sorting for \(Z_y\)). The following value is subtracted from data of Y

\begin{align}
&=\, \Big[\, 0.0 \, + \, cH_{0,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{0,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \,\, + \, cH_{0,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\,\Big] \\
&+\, \Big[ \, 0.0 \, + \, cH_{1,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{1,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \,\, + \, cH_{1,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_1(Z_y) \, \\
&+\, \Big[ \,0.0 \, + \, cH_{2,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{2,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \,\, + \, cH_{2,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_2(Z_y) \, \\
&+\, \Big[ \,0.0 \, + \, cH_{3,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{3,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \, \,+ \, cH_{3,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_3(Z_y) \, \\
&+\, \Big[ \,0.0 \, + \, cH_{4,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{4,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \,\, + \, cH_{4,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_4(Z_y) \, \\
&+\, \Big[ \,0.0 \, + \, cH_{5,1} \,H_1(Z_x) \,+\, cH_{5,2} \, H_2(Z_x) \, \,+ \, cH_{5,3} \,H_3(Z_x)\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \, H_5(Z_y) \, \\
\end{align} 

We similarly subtract the effect of X from W, V and U.

Please recall that we have removed the effect of X from data values of Y, W, V and U and these removed effect values are still in the form of slightly changed data. We calculate cross-moments between these removed effect data values of W and Y, V and U and from these cross-moments, we would calibrate the new coefficients of \(\left[\,Y\,-\,X\,|\,W\,\right](Z_y,Z_w)\). From respective cross-moments, we calibrate the coefficients of V and U given W.

Our \(Z_y\) remains the same. We again line up the data in order of increasing \(Z_y\) and subtract the effect of W (We could do this since we know the value of Z_y and Z_w associated with each data element).

Similarly we calibrate removed effect coefficients to V through removed effect cross-moments. And we continue with removing the effect of V and fitting the remaining data to coefficients of Y given U. And this would complete our calibration process.
