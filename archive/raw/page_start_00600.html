We give the Option payoff at the terminal time T on the asset grid. And we can later calculate the payoff at time T-1 using the transition probabilities since they are exactly known between any two points at grid at time T and at time T-1.
1.To calculate American option, we need to know expected pay off at time T-1 given payoff at time T which is calculated from transition probabilities.
2. The grid at T-1 also specifies the asset value that is needed for the American option pricing algorithm.
From 1 and 2, we can calculate the exercise boundary at time T-1 beyond which exercise is optimal. And the expected payoff is again calculated at time T-2 by using the transition probabilities between time T-1 and T-2 and given the expectation values of payoff at time T-1. Like for every step, exercise boundary is determined at time T-2 from the knowledge of expected payoff and the asset value at the Grid at time T-2.
Should be very straightforward,
