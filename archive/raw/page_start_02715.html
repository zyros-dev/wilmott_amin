Friends, I am trying to think aloud about handling conditional distributions in higher dimensions iteratively. 

Let us suppose, we have four random variables A, B, C and D. And we want to find the distribution of D conditional on A, B and C taking certain values. We are in a continuous distributions set up. We can first find conditional distributions of the whole universe of variables B, C and D on A in a two dimensional set up. Then we take these conditioned distributions and further find conditional distributions of  C|A and D|A given B|A. I believe it will give us C|(A and B) and  D|(A and B). Now we simply use two dimensional algorithm to find to condition D|(A and B) on C|(A and B) giving us D|(A and B and C). We will need intermediate steps to convert variables A|B into (A and B) using  P(A and B)=P(A|B)*P(B).  I think this can work for high dimensional conditional variables.
