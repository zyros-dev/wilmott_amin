Friends, perfectly rigorous way to add two arbitrary densities whose hermite polynomial representation has already been found is to find the moments of sum random variable. We can then apply our numerical moment matching algorithm to find the hermite polynomial representation of the sum density. When densities being added are perfectly uncorrelated, this is very simple and summation of cumulants would also equally work. We have done this several times when we were dealing with simulation of SDEs by Z-series/hermite -series.
When there are correlations between hermite polynomials of same order across two random variables, we will have to apply these correlations while finding expected values of moments from the series expansion. And then we would apply numerical method that finds the hermite polynomial expansion of the sum random variable by matching moments.

For example if we are given hermite/Z-series expansions of two random variables and we want to find their sum.

The first variable is
[$]Y=a_0 + a_1 \, Z_1 + a_2 \, {Z_1}^2 + a_3 \, {Z_1}^3 \, + \ldots \, + a_n \, {Z_1}^n [$]  Eq(A)
The second variable is
[$]X=b_0 + b_1 \, Z_2 + b_2 \, {Z_2}^2 + b_3 \, {Z_2}^3\, + \ldots \, + b_n \, {Z_2}^n [$]  Eq(B)

We want to remind the friends that for every Z-series in powers of Z, there is an equivalent Z-series in hermite polynomials given as
[$]Y=\mu_Y \, + ah_1 \, Z_1 + ah_2 \, (Z_1^2-1) + ah_{3} \, (Z_1^3-3 \, Z_1) + \ldots \, + ah_n \, H_n(Z_1) [$]
and
[$]X=\mu_X \, + bh_1 \, Z_2 + bh_2 \, (Z_2^2 \, -\, 1) + bh_3 \, (Z_2^3 \, - \, 3 \, Z_2)+ \ldots \, + bh_n \, H_n(Z_2) [$]

We will have to find mth moment as

[$]E\big[{(\, X \, + \, Y\, )}^m \, \big] [$]

Hermite series of X and Y are known and their correlations are also known so we know everything in the expansion of above expression we need to find its expected value. 
We will have to take expectations over powers of Z or powers of products of hermite polynomials depending upon which setting you expand the above expression. If there are non-zero correlations between hermite polynomials of various order., it would be advisable to expand as hermite polynomials and apply correlations between hermite polynomials of the same order across different variables. 
After finding the expectations of moments of the sum random variable, we would have to apply our numerical moment matching technique to find the hermite polynomial representation of the sum from the calculated moments. 
There are simpler heuristic methods to add two densities using their hermite polynomial representation but those methods are somewhat approximate and we can use them as finding a starting guess for the numerical technique that finds true hermite representation of the random variable given their moments. I am sure it will be a fast numerical routine.
