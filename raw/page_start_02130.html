Friends, I want to describe how to convert 2D Z-series Coefficients to 2D Hermite coefficients and relationship between derivatives of cross moments with respect to coefficients of Z-series and derivatives of cross moments with respect to coefficients of hermite series.

For a univariate Z-series up to 5th power of Z, we have

[$]\, X(Z) \, = \, a_1 \, + \, a_2 \, Z \, + \, a_3 \, Z^2 \, + \, a_4 \, Z^3 \, + \, a_5 \, Z^4 \,+ \, a_6 \, Z^5 \,[$]  Eq(1)

while its equivalent Hermite-series can be written as

[$]\,  X(Z) \, = \, ah_1 \,H_0(Z) + \, ah_2 \, H_1(Z) \, + \, ah_3 \, H_2(Z) \, + \, ah_4 \, H_3(Z) \, + \, ah_5 \, H_4(Z) \,+ \, ah_6 \, H_5(Z) \,[$]   Eq(2)

We know the relationship between coefficients is given as

[$]ah_1\,=\, a_1 \, + \, a_3 \, + 3\, a_5[$]
[$]ah_2\,=\, a_2 \, + 3\, a_4 \, + 15\, a_6[$]
[$]ah_3\,=\, a_3 \, +\, 6\, a_5 \, [$]
[$]ah_4\,=\, a_4\, +\, 10\, a_6 \, [$]
[$]ah_5\,=\, a_5 \, [$]
[$]ah_6\,=\, a_6 \,[$]  Eq(3)

While converting from 2D Z-series to 2D Hermite series, we have to apply the above procedure, for powers of Z1 first and then on powers of Z2 second.

If a 2D Z-series from 5 X 5 is given below

[$]X2(Z_1 \, , \, Z_2)\,=\, b_{1,1} \, + \, b_{2,1} \,Z_1 \,+\, b_{3,1} \, {Z_1}^2 \,+\, \ldots \, + \, b_{6,1} \,{Z_1}^5\, \\
+\, \Big[ \, b_{1,2} \, + \, b_{2,2} \, Z_1 \,+\, b_{3,2} \, {Z_1}^2 \,+\, \ldots \, + \, b_{6,2} \,{Z_1}^5\, \Big] \, Z_2 \, \\
+\, \Big[ \, b_{1,3} \, + \, b_{2,3} \,Z_1 \,+\, b_{3,3} \, {Z_1}^2 \,+\, \ldots \, + \, b_{6,3} \, {Z_1}^5\, \Big] \, {Z_2}^2 \, \\

In order to convert derivatives of cross moments with respect to coefficients of Z-series into derivatives of cross moments with respect to coefficients of hermite series, we use the formula

if [$]bh_1 \,= \,b_1\,+ \, b_3 \, + \,3\, b_5\,[$] Eq(7)

[$]\frac{\partial X}{\partial b_1}[$] and  [$]\frac{\partial X}{\partial b_3}[$] and [$]\frac{\partial X}{\partial b_5}[$] are given and in that case we have by 

differentiating Eq(7) with respect to X, we get

[$]{\Large \frac{\partial bh_1}{\partial X}\,= \,\frac{\partial b_1}{\partial X}\,+ \, \frac{\partial b_3}{\partial X} \, + \,3\, \frac{\partial b_5}{\partial X}}\,[$]

[$]{\LARGE \frac{\partial X}{\partial bh_1}\,=\, \frac{1}{ \,\frac{\partial b_1}{\partial X}\,+ \, \frac{\partial b_3}{\partial X} \, + \,3\, \frac{\partial b_5}{\partial X}}}\,[$]

where we know that

[$]{\Large \,\frac{\partial b_n}{\partial X}\,= \, \frac{1}{\frac{\partial X}{\partial b_n}} }\,[$]
