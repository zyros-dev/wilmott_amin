Friends, We can regress \(Ydecorr^2\) on squared Hermites of \(Z_x\) along the columns as suggested in the copied post. But we can also simultaneously regress  \(Ydecorr^2\) on squared Hermites of \(Z_y\) along the rows. As it turns out that if regressions are accurate, we can pin 36/64 coefficients of 2D Hermite-series using only 12/16 equations. If we can specify sum of coefficients of squared hermites of Zx along columns and sums of squared Hermites of Zy along rows, we only need these 12 equations to solve 6X6 coefficients and we need only 16 equations to solve 8X8 equations.

I will explain a bit more
A complete regression equation is of the form

\begin{align}
\left[{Ydecorr}^2|X\right](\tilde{Z}_y \, , \, Z_x)\, \,&=\, \, \Big[ \, {b_{1,0}}^2 \, + \, {b_{1,1}}^2 \,{H_1(Z_x)}^2 \,+\, {b_{1,2}}^2 \, {H_2(Z_x)}^2 \, + \, {b_{1,3}}^2 \,{H_3(Z_x)}^2\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \,{H_1(\tilde{Z}_y)}^2 \, \\
&+\,  \Big[ \, {b_{2,0}}^2 \, + \, {b_{2,1}}^2 \,{H_1(Z_x)}^2 \,+\, {b_{2,2}}^2 \, {H_2(Z_x)}^2 \, + \, {b_{2,3}}^2 \,{H_3(Z_x)}^2\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \,{H_2(\tilde{Z}_y)}^2 \, \\
&+\,  \Big[ \, {b_{3,0}}^2 \, + \, {b_{3,1}}^2 \,{H_1(Z_x)}^2 \,+\, {b_{3,2}}^2 \, {H_2(Z_x)}^2 \, + \, {b_{3,3}}^2 \,{H_3(Z_x)}^2\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \,{H_3(\tilde{Z}_y)}^2 \, \\
&+\,\,\,\ldots\, \\
\end{align} 

We can regress along columns of squared hermites of \(Z_x\) after expectations of squared hermites of \(Z_y\) as

\[\left[{Ydecorr}^2|X\right]( \, Z_x)\, \,=\Big[ \, {B_{0}}^2 \, + \, {B_{1}}^2 \,{H_1(Z_x)}^2 \,+\, {B_{2}}^2 \, {H_2(Z_x)}^2 \, + \, {B_{3}}^2 \,{H_3(Z_x)}^2\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \]
Where  \[{B_{0}}^2\,=\,{b_{1,0}}^2\,+\,2\,{b_{2,0}}^2\,+\,6\,{b_{3,0}}^2\,+ \ldots\]
 \[{B_{1}}^2\,=\,{b_{1,1}}^2\,+\,2\,{b_{2,1}}^2\,+\,6\,{b_{3,1}}^2\,+ \ldots\]
 \[{B_{2}}^2\,=\,{b_{1,2}}^2\,+\,2\,{b_{2,2}}^2\,+\,6\,{b_{3,2}}^2\,+ \ldots\]
and so on

But similarly, we can regress along rows of squared hermites of \(Z_y\) after expectations of squared hermites of \(Z_x\) as

\[\left[{Ydecorr}^2|X\right]( \, Z_y)\, \,=\Big[ \, {C_{0}}^2 \, + \, {C_{1}}^2 \,{H_1(Z_y)}^2 \,+\, {C_{2}}^2 \, {H_2(Z_y)}^2 \, + \, {C_{3}}^2 \,{H_3(Z_y)}^2\,\,+\,\ldots\, \Big] \]
Where  \[{C_{0}}^2\,=\,{b_{0,1}}^2\,+\,2\,{b_{0,2}}^2\,+\,6\,{b_{0,3}}^2\,+ \ldots\]
 \[{C_{1}}^2\,=\,{b_{1,1}}^2\,+\,2\,{b_{1,2}}^2\,+\,6\,{b_{1,3}}^2\,+ \ldots\]
 \[{C_{2}}^2\,=\,{b_{2,1}}^2\,+\,2\,{b_{2,2}}^2\,+\,6\,{b_{2,3}}^2\,+ \ldots\]
and so on

It turns out that if regressions are accurate, we can solve for all of the 6X6 coefficients of 2D Hermite-series from knowledge of \(B_1,\ldots\,,\,B_6\) and \(C_1,\ldots\,,\,C_6\). You can verify this by actually writing a matrix and solving for a 6X6 matrix so that rows and columns are summed to values of regressions from squared hermites of Zx and Zy.

Even if it is not hundred percent accurate, this result can be helpful in giving a very good initial seed value that can be input to iterative program with constraints on both rows and columns of equations.

Another thing is that we only get squared coefficients from both the regressions. After solution of squared values of all the 6X6 coefficients, we can add them one by one after taking square-root and seeing which sign decreases our objective function and which sign increases the objective function so we can choose a proper sign of the coefficients of \(Ydecorr\).

Just a quick post to mention even though we can directly regress square of Ydecorr on squared hermites of Zy or Zx, it is also possible to regress Ydecorr on (non-squared) hermites  of Zx or Zy and find coefficients of squared hermites from the coefficients of non-squared hermites. It is because span of first five squared hermites is lesser than or equal to the span of first ten (non-squared) hermites and they can be represented by non-squared hermites. So we can directly do a regression of Ydecorr on non-squared hermites that could possibly be more accurate and then construct coefficients of squared hermites from coefficients of non-squared straight hermites.

Non-squared hermite polynomials might be helpful since they are orthogonal while squared hermites are not orthogonal.
