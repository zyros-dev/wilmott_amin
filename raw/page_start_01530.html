Friends, here is the progress for today. I have written a small function that starts with coefficient of first power of Z in the Z-series and I set it to 1 for unit variance. Then I continue to calibrate one higher coefficient of Z-series (one by one) so that the next moment is matched. For example I calculate the value of each moment with zero coefficient and if with zero relevant coefficient, model moment value is smaller than target moment, I assign it a small positive value and smooth for 3-4 iterations until the model moment matches the target moment. Similarly if with zero coefficient, the target moment is smaller than model moment, I assign the coefficient a small negative value and smooth for 3-4 iterations until the particular moment is matched. After matching each coefficient (one after the other), I re-scale the variance to one each time. Starting from 1st coefficient variance is matched. 2nd coefficient matches skew. 3rd coefficient matches fourth moment and so on till seventh coefficient for eighth moment. Every time, I rescale the variance to one, the earlier calibration is slightly off but really remains quite reasonably in line.
This recursive matching of coefficients works far better than the initial seed we were getting from solution of linear equation of the polynomial densities. 
Later the initial seed goes to smoothing method. I still want to improve the smoothing method for a day or two so that it works very well. And then finally we solve the cumulant equations with Newton method as before.
